# Finite RGO program and trajectory semantics

On explicit states (b,u,k,h) with nonnegative precision b,center u,step count k and a sequence h storing the k most recent observations, take a measurable positive current-state variance a and a positive terminal precision threshold. Construct the actual precision-weighted update, increment k and prepend the observation. For arbitrary actual observation and terminal Markov kernels Q,L, construct a Markov state transition which is identity on terminal states and is the Q-pushforward under that explicit update elsewhere. Construct measurable probability output kernels R_n with R_0=L and R_(n+1)(s)=L(s) on terminal states, otherwise the integral of R_n(updated(s,y)) against Q(s). Construct the finite full trajectory law and prove its terminal-output marginal equals this recursive output. Use StateDependentRGO to construct actual target and ideal observation kernels; for Q=H and L=T prove every finite output R_n(s)=T(s), deriving posterior consistency from the actual update rather than assuming recovery. No deterministic schedule is imposed.

Finite capped unfolding with explicit absorbing terminal branch. At the cap the remaining call is represented by supplied L. The theorem does not prove that the cap exceeds the source stopping depth, implement FORS or the approximate smoothed sampler, or prove accuracy or expected query cost. Ideal L=T is an oracle comparison only. State stores finite history via count and padded sequence; source reference-point and accuracy parameter validity, and a=(eta+tau)/beta_A, remain explicit later instantiations.

Use a concrete product state, with count and padded observation sequence. Measurability of prepend follows coordinatewise. Build the terminal set from measurable precision and threshold; use Kernel.piecewise with identity on that set. The output composition with transition powers must be proved to satisfy the stated conditional recursion and to agree with a genuine partialTraj law. For ideal recovery, derive T(updated(s,y))=posterior(s,y) from explicit parameter algebra and StateDependentRGO, then prove recovery at every finite depth including absorbed states. Never assume the desired invariance.
