It checks pinned external retrieval memory, source and semantic contracts, Discrete Sampling and MCMC regressions, the canonical Lean gate, Frontier Cells, Chapter 1 evidence, full source-derived site generation, static-site validation, browser interaction over all discrete/MCMC readers and graph views, and an identity-leak scan on the final deployable site.
