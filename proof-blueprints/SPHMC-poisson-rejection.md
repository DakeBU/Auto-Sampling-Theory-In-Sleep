# Actual Poisson rejection

For a measurable parameter space S, a Markov proposal kernel Q:S to X, auxiliary probability Xi on A, jointly measurable W:S times X times A to Real bounded in [-B,B] for a fixed B>0, and a default output x0, construct the actual independent one-attempt law from Q(s), Poisson(2B), an iid Xi stream and Uniform[0,1]. Read exactly the first N auxiliary coordinates and accept with the product of (B+W)/(2B). Prove the conditional acceptance probability exp(w(s,x)-B), w(s,x)=integral W(s,x,z) Xi(dz), and the accepted submeasure Q(s).withDensity(ofReal(exp(w(s,.)-B))). Its mass p(s) is positive and at least exp(-2B). On an actual iid sequence of whole attempts, construct the total first-success output with default x0 on never-success; prove that never-success has zero mass, the preceding-failure tail is (1-p(s))^m, and the output law is Q(s).tilted(w(s,.)), forming a measurable Markov kernel in s. Prove the actual full-batch accumulated auxiliary-query count including the successful attempt has expected value 2B/p(s)<=2B*exp(2B). Do not assume independence of the current acceptance event and its Poisson count.

Actual bounded-estimator Poisson rejection semantics and auxiliary-query cost only. A fixed parameter-independent auxiliary law and fixed B are explicit inputs. Clipping an actual gradient estimator, identifying its mean with the target log tilt, normalized Renyi approximation, approximate proximal reference points and gradient cost per auxiliary evaluation remain separate. An early-short-circuit implementation inherits an upper bound by the full-batch cost, not an equality. Parameterized output measurability must be proved, not inferred from fiberwise existence. No terminal accuracy or full paper result follows from this mechanism alone.
